BEFORE YOUR FIRST PAPER TRADE

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is this real trading?+

No. PaperFills simulates supported trading actions with a paper balance. The market chart stays real, but paper trades do not move its candles. Fills and fees are estimates, not a guarantee of real execution.

Do I need to fund a wallet?+

No deposit is needed for paper trading. Use a separate, unfunded Axiom account for practice. PaperFills intercepts supported controls and is not a security boundary; disabling paper mode restores Axiom’s live controls.

Does it support multiple wallets?+

Yes, on supported Solana trading screens. Buys use the displayed amount per selected paper wallet, with fees charged separately. Percentage sells use each wallet’s own holding. Open the trading wallet selector after a full reload; automatic wallet-group rotation is not supported.

Which markets can I practice on?+

Supported Axiom markets on Solana use a SOL paper balance. Robinhood Chain has a separate ETH paper balance. This does not mean general support for Ethereum, Base, or BNB.

Where are my trades saved?+

In extension-local storage in this Chrome profile, not ordinary website cache. It persists across browser restarts. Export a backup before uninstalling, resetting, or moving to a different profile. Read about data and privacy.
